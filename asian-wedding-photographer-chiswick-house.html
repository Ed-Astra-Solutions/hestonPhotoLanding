Venue Guide · Chiswick, West London

Asian Wedding Photographer at Chiswick House and Gardens

A neo-Palladian villa with formal eighteenth-century gardens, and one of the most photogenic settings in West London for both weddings and pre-wedding shoots. We have shot both here.

Photographing at Chiswick House and Gardens

Architecture that does the work for you

The classical facade, the conservatory and the formal gardens give a range of backdrops within a short walk of each other — useful when the portrait window is tight.

Best light

Late afternoon is the window. The formal gardens face into good evening light, and the villa's stonework warms up rather than going flat.

Pre-wedding shoots

Chiswick House is one of our regular pre-wedding locations. It photographs as somewhere considerably grander than the time it takes to get there from central London.

Access and permits

Chiswick House and Gardens is a managed heritage site with its own rules on commercial photography. We handle permissions in advance so nobody is negotiating with a warden on the day.
